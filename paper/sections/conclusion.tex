\section{Conclusion}

We introduced TreemapMix, a Dirichlet-controlled $K$-way image-mixing
augmentation that composes multiple labeled images into area-proportional
treemap layouts while preserving the training sample count. By generating
cyclic views of each source group, TreemapMix exposes each source image to
different region sizes and rank positions. The same composition supports two
complementary supervision objectives: area-weighted soft cross-entropy for probability
matching and Plackett-Luce ranking for area-induced ordinal supervision.

Empirically, TreemapMix improves ImageNet-1K classification under matched
training recipes. TreemapMix-SCE gives the strongest calibration, while
TreemapMix-PL gives the strongest Top-1 accuracy and improves area-logit
alignment. COCO transfer experiments further suggest that TreemapMix-pretrained
representations remain useful for detection and instance segmentation.
The accuracy-calibration pattern also extends to the additional ImageNet-1K
architectures and largely to CIFAR-100. On ImageNet-LT, TreemapMix-PL improves
accuracy, while TreemapMix-SCE does not retain its calibration advantage.

Our experiments use groups with distinct source labels. Together, the results
show how a shared spatial composition can support both probability matching
and ranking supervision, opening directions for adaptive grouping, automatic
schedules for $K$ and $\alpha$, and extensions to contrastive or
self-supervised learning.
